# Audio Continuity

Global audio reference area for `This Is Your Life`.

## Current recovered recurring language
- television/CRT activation sounds are part of S01E01 production language;
- heartbeat, breath, silence and environmental sound are repeatedly used as narrative devices;
- the Announcer/Voice requires a consistent identity once the final voice bible is locked;
- episode-specific sound design belongs inside the episode's production/audio package.

Audio production may intensify emotion, but it must not add story facts that are absent from canon.
