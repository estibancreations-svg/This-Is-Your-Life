## Season 1

### S01E01 — The Foundation of Lies
- Subject: **Sarah Jenkins**
- Status: Confirmed
- Core device: vintage television / life review / judgment / second chance
- Confirmed ending: Sarah returns before the life-defining criminal path, the men involved are exposed/arrested, and a later future shows Sarah in a park with a son who did not exist in the original timeline. Across the street, her former house is for sale; she does not recognize it because that corrupt life never occurred in the corrected timeline.

### S01E02 — Living Gallery
- Subject: name still to recover
- Status: Confirmed story; identity and original full narrative still under recovery
- Core arc: a man dies believing he harmed or negatively influenced people. He asks to return and leave a better impression. He is permitted to return but cannot speak. He wakes in a hospital with his sisters. They care for him and take him to their gallery, where his artwork is displayed. He invested so much emotion in the pieces that viewers literally feel the emotion contained in the work.
- Drive folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1DVjvBE89S37J4BK0oG9o1aGOEBzrxCJ_

### S01E03 — See and Feel — RECOVERY
- Subject: serial killer; name still to recover
- Status: creator-confirmed canon; exact original source not yet recovered
- Core arc: a serial killer who murdered women is tracked by a squad later revealed as angels. The television confronts him with his crimes. He repeatedly chooses to watch. The Fates issue the judgment **“See and Feel.”** He becomes each victim approximately five minutes before her murder and experiences what she experienced, including seeing his former self as the killer. The experience repeats across the victims.
