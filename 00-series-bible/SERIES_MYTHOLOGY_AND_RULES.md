# Series Mythology and Rules

This document records only recovered or creator-confirmed mechanics. Missing details remain open.

## The Television
- A recurring judgment/confrontation device.
- It presents a subject with **“This Is Your Life”** and forces confrontation with the truth of the subject's life.
- It is not merely nostalgic playback; it functions as evidence, mirror, and trigger for judgment.
- The exact relationship between the physical television, the Announcer/Voice, the Fates, and any angelic agents remains under recovery.

## The Announcer / Voice
- Speaks through or in association with the television.
- Calls the subject by name and frames the life review.
- In recovered production material, the voice can challenge, pronounce judgment, and acknowledge a return/assignment.
- Do not automatically identify the Voice as one of the Fates unless a source explicitly confirms it.

## The Fates
- Higher adjudicating entities in creator-confirmed canon.
- They interpret or enforce the meaning of choices.
- Choice wording may not mean what the subject assumes it means.
- In the serial-killer story, the Fates' judgment is **“See and Feel.”**

## Angels / Agents
- Angelic beings can operate physically within a subject's world.
- In the serial-killer story, the squad tracking the killer is ultimately revealed as angelic.
- Their names, hierarchy, powers, and relationship to the Fates are still under recovery.

## Choice Framework
Recovered source material includes:
- **Relive**
- **Move On**
- **Sacrifice**

The framework appears to test character rather than simply grant a literal wish. Consequences can reveal what the subject actually chose at a moral level.

## Altered Timeline Rule
S01E01 establishes that a returned subject can change a critical choice and create a substantially different life. In the corrected timeline, Sarah Jenkins can live a future she experiences as her only life and no longer recognizes the house central to her original corrupt timeline.

## Anti-Invention Rule
Do not invent:
- names of Fates or angels;
- exact cosmology;
- universal choice wording for every episode;
- number of victims in `See and Feel`;
- rules of memory retention after return;
- episode numbering beyond what is currently creator-confirmed.

Add these only when an original source is recovered or the creator explicitly canonizes them.
