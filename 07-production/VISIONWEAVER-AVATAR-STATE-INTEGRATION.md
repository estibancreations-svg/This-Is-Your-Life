## Anthology production use

Pin each episode character's canonical detail and approved appearance, voice, performance and coverage into the A Cast Board. Preserve age/body/hair/wardrobe/injury/voice changes as explicit scoped state revisions. Keep episode-specific characters and recurring identities distinct.

Sound source, arrival, reaction and camera emphasis have separate records on the shared clock. Enclosed/open/tunnel/vacuum behavior depends on actual medium and boundaries.

Children's style and grade profiles do not apply to the anthology unless the Architect explicitly selects them. Share performance/camera/contact/text/handoff methods while preserving creator-confirmed story canon.

## Change and source rules

Pinned versions and immutable asset hashes govern handoff. Record cause/scope/before/after/approver/source and targeted stale descendants. Preserve prior approved states and source uncertainty. Approved appearance outranks descriptive shorthand; ethnicity, language, accent and identity are separate. Do not overwrite corrected Black/fat representation through stylization or an older text description.

The full-coverage expansion is eight directions × eight configured heights = 64 full-body slots plus separate details; exact camera calibration remains unresolved. Keep older approved 32 masters and wardrobe-only 16 variants usable, and do not certify archived 24 studies as full coverage.
