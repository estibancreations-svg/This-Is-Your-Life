# VisionWeaver Handoff Rules — This Is Your Life

## Purpose
VisionWeaver is the production intelligence layer for translating locked story canon into scenes, character/environment continuity, prompts, audio direction, generated shots, extensions, stitching and review.

## Canon boundary
VisionWeaver may:
- break stories into acts, scenes and shots;
- create character and environment locks;
- convert approved story beats into Runway-ready prompts;
- design audio, camera, lighting and continuity plans;
- maintain asset IDs and visual references;
- support extension/stitching workflows.

VisionWeaver may not:
- invent missing canon and silently mark it final;
- merge `Crossroads of Identity` characters or story material into this property;
- replace creator-confirmed endings because a production payload omitted them;
- treat a production protocol as proof of a missing story source.

## Preferred production flow
Story canon → emotional arc → character bible → location bible → object/motif/artwork bible → scene cards → shot cards → reference images → generation prompts → generated shots → extensions → edit/stitch → sound → continuity review.

## Asset naming
Use stable IDs such as:
`TYL_S01E01_SC04_SH02_STROBE_MARBLE`

The goal is one durable identifier across storyboard, prompt, render, audio, continuity ledger and final edit.

## Current production status
Runway production remains secondary to source recovery and canon organization. `See and Feel` and the Living Gallery protagonist identity must not be fabricated to accelerate rendering.
