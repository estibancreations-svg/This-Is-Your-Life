# S01E02 — Living Gallery

**Working title:** Living Gallery  
**Subject:** name still under recovery  
**Status:** confirmed story; original full narrative not yet recovered

## Creator-confirmed story
A man dies believing he was a bad influence on the people he touched. He asks for another chance so he can leave a better impression. He is allowed to return, but under the condition that he cannot speak. He wakes in a hospital surrounded by his sisters. As they begin caring for him, they take him to their art gallery. His artwork is displayed there.

The defining rule of the work: he invested so much emotion into each piece that people who experience the work literally feel the emotion carried within it.
