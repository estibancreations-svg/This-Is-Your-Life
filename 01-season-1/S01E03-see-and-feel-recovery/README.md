## Creator-confirmed canon
A serial killer murdered women across a period of years. A squad that appears to be tracking him is ultimately revealed to be angelic. The television presents the truth of his crimes. Rather than recoiling from what he did, he attempts to view the murders repeatedly.

The Fates' judgment is:

> See and Feel.

The punishment changes the meaning of “relive.” He does not return as himself. He becomes each victim approximately five minutes before he murdered her. He sees his own former self arrive, attack, and kill the body he now occupies. He experiences what the victim experienced, with sensation and emotional consequence amplified. The process repeats across his victims.
