# S01E01 — The Foundation of Lies

**Subject:** Sarah Jenkins  
**Status:** confirmed canon / production reconstruction in progress

## Core story
Sarah's outwardly immaculate life is exposed as a structure built on harm. A vintage television enters her home and activates the `This Is Your Life` judgment mechanism. Her past is replayed, including the criminal choices that helped create her apparent success and the damage inflicted on others.

Creator-confirmed canon extends beyond the recovered 12-scene production payload: Sarah ultimately returns before the life-defining criminal path, exposes/helps arrest the men involved, and creates a different future. Later, she is in a park with a son who did not exist in the original timeline. Across the street stands her former house with a `For Sale` sign. Sarah does not recognize it because the corrupt timeline never became her lived reality.
