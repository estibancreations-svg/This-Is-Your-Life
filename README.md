# This Is Your Life

Private creative-development repository for **This Is Your Life**, an anthology series under EstibanCreations.

## Canon rule
Original story material and creator-confirmed canon outrank later production mappings. VisionWeaver, Runway, Gemini, Claude, and other production documents may interpret canon for production, but may not silently rewrite it.

## Repository structure
- `00-series-bible/` — master canon, mythology, rules, recovery ledger
- `01-season-1/` — Season 1 episode records
- `02-season-2/` — Season 2 episode records
- `03-characters/` — recurring and episode character indexes
- `04-locations/` — recurring and episode location indexes
- `05-artwork-visual-bibles/` — artwork manifests and visual continuity references
- `06-scripts-outlines/` — repo-safe script and outline indexes
- `07-production/` — VisionWeaver/Runway production specifications
- `08-audio/` — voice, sound, music and audio continuity references
- `09-source-records/` — source manifests and Google Drive pointers
- `99-archive/` — superseded or duplicate repo-safe records
